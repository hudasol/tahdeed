# Instructions for volunteers

Thank you for helping. This takes about 10 minutes. Please read `CONSENT.md` first (the form shows a summary too).

## What to do

1. Open the form file you were given (`form_<your code>.html`) in any browser. It works without internet.
2. You will see **five places**: a name (English and Arabic) and a category. Nothing else. No map, no address.
3. For each place, write **how you would tell a taxi or delivery driver to get there**, in your own words, the way you would say it on a phone call.
4. Write in whatever way is natural to you: **Arabic, English, Arabizi** (Arabic in Latin letters and digits, such as `3ala yameen`) **or a mix**. Do not translate or "clean up" your wording.
5. Click **Download my responses** and send the saved file to the organiser.

## Tips

- Say what you would really say: the area, the gate or entrance, what is opposite or next to it, the nearest landmark, the roundabout or the turn.
- A short answer is fine. Perfect addresses are **not** needed and you must **not** look anything up or open a map.
- If you do not know a place, tick **I don't know this place**. Do not guess.
- If you would give different directions on the phone to a driver who speaks Arabic versus English, write the one you would use most often.

## Please do not write

- Home addresses, villa or flat numbers ("my house", "villa 12"), personal phone numbers, email addresses, social media names, or any person's name. **Public places only.**

The form warns you if a text looks like one of these. If it is a false alarm (for example a mall's own landmark), you can still download.

## Two invented examples

These places are **made up and are not on your list**; they only show the style.

**Place: "Example Mall" (shopping mall, invented)**

> Go to Example Mall, but not the main entrance. Use the side gate on the street with the pharmacy, it is opposite the big blue petrol station, in Al Wahda. The taxi stand is right there.

**Place: "Sample Station" (metro station, invented)**

> Roh lel station el qadeem, ba3d el dawwar el thani, el bawwaba el shamaliya gedam el bakery. Law ma la2ayt, 3ala yameen el masjed el kabeer.

The first example is English, the second is Arabizi. Yours can be in any language or a mix.
